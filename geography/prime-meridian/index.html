The Prime Meridian (0° Longitude)

The Prime Meridian is the reference line of 0 degrees longitude passing through the Royal Observatory in Greenwich, London. It establishes Greenwich Mean Time (GMT / UTC) and divides Earth into the Eastern and Western Hemispheres.

Geographic Dimensions & Position

Exact Coordinates0° 00' 00" Longitude
Circumference / Length20,003.93 km (pole to pole)
Scientific Significance

Defined internationally at the 1884 International Meridian Conference in Washington, D.C. It is the baseline from which all global time zones and longitude coordinates are measured.
