## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-16

### Added
- **Core Geospatial Engine:** Karney WGS84 ellipsoidal geodesic algorithms via GeographicLib, Great-Circle spherical calculations, Rhumb lines, midpoint, and surface integrals.
- **Universal Coordinate Converter:** Two-way converter across DD, DMS, DMM, UTM (Proj4), MGRS, Plus Codes, and Geohash.
- **12 Launch Tools:** Map Radius, Map Area Calculator, Distance Between Places, Latitude/Longitude Finder, Address to Coordinates, Coordinates to Address, GPS Coordinate Converter, Elevation Finder, What Is My Elevation, Drive Time Map (Isochrones), Pin Drop Map, CSV to Map, and Map Drawer.
- **Printable Blank Map Library:** SVG vector rendering and print-ready PDF download hub for World, Continents, USA, and US States.
- **US State & County Hub:** Directory of all 50 US states with searchable Census demographic tables, FIPS lookups, and county boundaries.
- **Content Authority Layer:** Comprehensive mathematical methodology, verified data sources, editorial policy, glossary, 4 pillar guides, and the Metro Detour Factor study with downloadable CSV dataset.
- **AEO & SEO Architecture:** Server-rendered definitions, worked mathematical examples, Schema.org JSON-LD structured data, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, and `llms.txt`.
