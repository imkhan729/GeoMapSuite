KML to GPX: Convert Google Earth Routes for GPS Devices

Understand KML-to-GPX geometry mapping, elevation handling, and the limitations of moving visual Earth layers into GPS data.

KML-to-GPX conversion maps KML Point placemarks to GPX waypoints and LineString paths to GPX tracks. Coordinates and optional elevations can transfer, but Google Earth styles, folders, tours, polygons, overlays, and NetworkLinks do not have direct GPX equivalents.

Core Concepts & Learning Progression

01

Identify KML geometry

Separate Point placemarks from LineString routes and polygon boundaries.

02

Map to GPX elements

Use wpt for standalone points and trk/trkseg for recorded or planned paths.

03

Check elevation

A third KML coordinate value can become GPX ele, but altitude mode and terrain meaning may differ.

04

Load and verify

Open the GPX in a compatible GPS or map app and compare it with the original KML.

Essential Technical Definitions

GPX waypoint

A standalone GPS point represented by a GPX wpt element.

GPX track

An ordered sequence of trackpoints represented by trk and trkseg elements.

KML LineString

An ordered KML coordinate path that can map to a GPX track.

Elevation mode

The KML rule describing how altitude is interpreted relative to ground, sea level, or a clamp.
