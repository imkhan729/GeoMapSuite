Direct Answer & Core Functionality

The Find Cities in Radius tool identifies all incorporated municipalities, state capitals, and urban population centers located within a custom radial distance buffer of any chosen city, address, or geographic coordinate. Utilizing WGS84 ellipsoidal geodesics (Karney algorithm), it calculates straight-line distances, 16-point compass directions, and aggregate population sums, with instant export options for CSV data sheets and formatted briefing lists.

How Radius City Calculations Work

The Find Cities in Radius engine uses Charles Karney’s algorithms on the WGS84 Reference Ellipsoid (semi-major axis a = 6,378,137 m, flattening f = 1/298.257223563). Unlike simple flat Euclidean buffers which distort by up to 25% at mid and high latitudes, ellipsoidal geodesy preserves exact surface distances and true azimuth bearings across continental scales.

Understanding Your Results & Practical Interpretation

Straight-Line Geodesic Distance vs. Driving Travel Time

This tool computes exact ellipsoidal straight-line distance across the Earth’s curved surface. In commercial real estate, broadcast licensing, and territory mapping, geographic radial buffers provide the legally binding standard benchmark.

Population Sums & Urban Density Analysis

The aggregate population metric sums the official resident populations of all matched incorporated municipalities within the buffer, giving immediate insights into the market depth and customer density of your territory.

16-Point Cardinal Compass Bearings

Each matched city includes an exact forward bearing angle in degrees [0, 360) and 16-point cardinal compass direction (e.g. N, NNE, NE, ENE, E), allowing you to easily categorize regional markets into directional sectors.

Export & Spreadsheet Integration

The "Export CSV" button generates a comprehensive spreadsheet complete with city names, states, counties, distances in miles and kilometers, bearings, coordinates, and populations, ready for instant import into GIS platforms, Excel, or Google Sheets.

Mathematical Methodology & Geodetic Accuracy

WGS84 Ellipsoidal Geodesics & Spatial Proximity Indexing

Distances between the center origin coordinates and candidate city centroids are computed along the WGS84 reference ellipsoid (semi-major axis $a = 6,378,137\text{ m}$, flattening $f = 1/298.257223563$) using Charles Karney’s exact geodesic algorithms. Azimuth bearings are calculated as initial forward geodesics.

s_{12} = a \int_{\sigma_1}^{\sigma_2} \sqrt{1 - k^2 \sin^2 \sigma} \, d\sigma \quad \text{where } s_{12} \le R_{\text{radius}}

Limitations & Boundary Conditions

  • •Calculates straight-line "as the crow flies" geodesic distance, which does not account for road driving network routing or traffic.
  • •Reflects incorporated municipal boundaries and census designated places; unincorporated rural areas are represented by their nearest municipal administrative seat.
  • •Population counts reflect official US Census Bureau decennial metrics and recent municipal census estimates.

Troubleshooting & Geographic Edge Cases

Why does my search return fewer cities than expected in western or rural states?

Western states like Wyoming, Montana, and Nevada have vast land areas with widely dispersed cities. A 50-mile radius in the Mountain West may only contain 1 or 2 incorporated cities, whereas the same radius in the Northeast or Midwest can contain dozens.

How do I toggle between Miles and Kilometers?

Use the Miles / KM unit switcher located directly above the radius distance slider. The radius calculation, map buffer polygon, and result tables update instantly.

How can I filter out small towns and only see major cities?

Use the "Min Population" dropdown to set a threshold such as 50,000+, 100,000+, or 500,000+. This automatically filters the list to only include larger municipal hubs.

Can I set a custom origin point that is not in the city catalog?

Yes. Simply click anywhere on the interactive map to place a custom origin pin at that exact latitude and longitude. The tool will immediately recalculate all nearby cities within your specified radius.
