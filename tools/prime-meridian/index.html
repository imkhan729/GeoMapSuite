Interactive map of the Greenwich Prime Meridian (0° Longitude), GMT/UTC baseline, and countries crossed.

Direct Answer & Core Functionality

The Prime Meridian is the reference line at 0° longitude. It passes through Greenwich, London, and is the origin from which east and west longitudes are measured.

Understanding Your Results & Practical Interpretation

Longitude origin

All geographic longitudes are measured east or west from 0°. The line is a reference convention, not a visible physical feature.

Greenwich and UTC

Greenwich Mean Time is historically associated with the meridian; modern UTC is maintained by international time standards and is not determined by one observatory.

Troubleshooting & Geographic Edge Cases

Is the Prime Meridian exactly at the Greenwich line?

The internationally used geodetic reference meridian is slightly east of the historic Greenwich transit instrument; maps may show either convention.

Does it determine UTC?

It is linked historically to GMT, while UTC is maintained by international atomic timekeeping.
