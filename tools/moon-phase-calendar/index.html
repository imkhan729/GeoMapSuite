Accuracy & Benchmark Standard

Geodesic Precision vs. Competitor Mapping Approaches

Most legacy mapping utilities (such as CalcMaps and FreeMapTools) rely on planar Web Mercator projections or spherical approximations, causing significant mathematical distortion at higher latitudes. GeoMap Suite computes exact ellipsoidal geodesics on the WGS84 reference ellipsoid.

Step-by-Step Mathematical Process

  2. Compute illumination fraction: 0.998 (~100% illuminated).
  3. Evaluate lunar cycle age: Day 14.76 of 29.53 synodic cycle.
Practical Takeaway: A full moon occurs when the Moon is on the opposite side of Earth from the Sun.

Troubleshooting & Geographic Edge Cases

Why is a Quarter Moon 50% illuminated?

A "First Quarter" or "Third Quarter" moon refers to the Moon being one-quarter of the way through its 29.5-day orbit, at which point exactly half (50%) of the visible disk is illuminated.
