MGRS Coordinate Converter

Convert coordinates to NATO Military Grid Reference System (MGRS) strings and decode MGRS to Latitude/Longitude.

Direct Answer & Core Functionality

The MGRS Converter translates standard GPS coordinates into the Military Grid Reference System (MGRS) alphanumeric grid used by NATO armed forces. It breaks down the coordinate into Grid Zone Designation (GZD), 100,000-meter Square Identification, and metric Easting/Northing numerical coordinates at 1-meter to 10-kilometer precision.

Accuracy & Benchmark Standard

Geodesic Precision vs. Competitor Mapping Approaches

Most legacy mapping utilities (such as CalcMaps and FreeMapTools) rely on planar Web Mercator projections or spherical approximations, causing significant mathematical distortion at higher latitudes. GeoMap Suite computes exact ellipsoidal geodesics on the WGS84 reference ellipsoid.

Step-by-Step Mathematical Process

  1. Determine UTM Zone & Band: 18S.
  2. Determine 100km Square Identifier: UJ.
  3. Extract 5-digit Easting (32008) and 5-digit Northing (04419) within the 100km square.
  4. Assemble alphanumeric MGRS string: 18SUJ3200804419.
Practical Takeaway: MGRS enables concise, unambiguous communication of geographic positions over voice radio without decimal points.

Understanding Your Results & Practical Interpretation

MGRS String Structure

The string "18SUJ3200804419" consists of: GZD "18S" (6°×8° zone), Square ID "UJ" (100km×100km cell), Easting "32008" (32,008m from cell west edge), Northing "04419" (4,419m from cell south edge).

Mathematical Methodology & Geodetic Accuracy

NATO Military Grid Reference System Specification

Based on UTM and UPS coordinate projections, divided into 100,000-meter grid squares with lettering schema defined in STANAG 2211.

MGRS = [GZD] + [100k Square ID] + [Easting Digits] + [Northing Digits]

Troubleshooting & Geographic Edge Cases

What does a 6-digit MGRS mean?

A 6-digit coordinate (e.g. 18SUJ320044) provides 100-meter precision, omitting the last two digits of Easting and Northing.

Frequently Asked Questions

MGRS is built directly upon UTM. It simplifies UTM zone coordinates by replacing the large numerical Zone and False Easting/Northing offsets with an alphanumeric 100,000-meter square designator (e.g. "UJ"), producing concise coordinate strings.
