1. **Great Circle Route (Orthodrome)**:
   - **Definition**: The intersection of a sphere with a plane that passes through the exact center of the Earth.
   - **Key Advantage**: It is the mathematically **shortest possible distance** between two points on the globe.

   - **Key Advantage**: It allows for **constant compass heading navigation**. A ship captain can set a magnetic compass to 072° and maintain that exact heading without adjusting the helm.
