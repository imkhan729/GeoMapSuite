Frequently Asked Questions

People Also Ask About Cartography Tutorials

Frequently asked questions about geographic calculations, map projections, and data formats.

QWhat topics do the GeoMap Suite cartographic guides cover?

Our tutorials provide practical, formula-backed explanations of map radius circles, geodesic distance vs driving route detour factors, coordinate reference datums, polygon area calculations, and vector file standards.

QHow do you calculate the true surface area of a radius ring on a map?

While flat geometry uses $A = \pi r^2$, on the curved Earth this simple formula distorts at high latitudes. Our guides demonstrate how to compute ellipsoidal surface area using 64-vertex geodesic polygons on the WGS84 datum.

QWhat is the difference between statute miles and nautical miles?

A statute mile is 5,280 feet (1,609.344 meters) used on land in the US and UK. A nautical mile is exactly 1,852 meters (1.1508 statute miles), historically defined as one minute of arc along any meridian for marine and air navigation.

QWhy does a straight flight path appear curved on a standard web map?

Because flat maps (Web Mercator) project a 3D sphere onto a 2D plane, the shortest distance between two points (a great circle geodesic) curves toward the poles, making transoceanic flight routes look bowed northward.

QHow do I choose between GeoJSON, KML, and Shapefiles for a project?

Use GeoJSON for web applications, MapLibre, and Leaflet APIs; choose KML/KMZ for 3D terrain visualization in Google Earth; and use ESRI Shapefiles when delivering data to legacy desktop GIS software suites.

QAre the worked examples on GeoMap Suite reproducible in QGIS and Python?

Yes. Every mathematical equation and worked tutorial in our guides uses open geodetic standards that can be replicated in QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, Python GeoPandas, and Turf.js.
