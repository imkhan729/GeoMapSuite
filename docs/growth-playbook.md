# 90-Day Organic Growth & Optimization Playbook

## 1. Growth Strategy Overview
The GeoSuite organic growth loop relies on three compounding engines:
1. **High-Intent Tool Utility:** Direct utility pages capturing navigational, informational, and calculation intent (e.g. radius on map, distance between coordinates).
2. **Programmatic Geographic Clusters:** High-density programmatic pages covering all 50 US states, counties, and global geographic lines.
3. **Citation-Worthy Research & Free Vectors:** Original empirical studies (such as the Metro Detour Factor Study) and high-resolution downloadable blank vector maps earning organic backlinks.

---

## 2. 90-Day Execution Calendar

### Days 1–30: Indexation & Technical Stability
- **Week 1:** Verify GSC and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit `/sitemap.xml`; verify 100% crawl rate without 4xx/5xx errors.
- **Week 2:** Monitor Core Web Vitals (LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1) across mobile and desktop.
- **Week 3:** Review initial impressions in GSC Performance report; verify SERP rich snippet display (JSON-LD FAQ and WebApplication snippets).
- **Week 4:** Conduct second crawl audit using open-source crawler to ensure zero orphan pages or broken internal links.

### Days 31–60: Content Optimization & Long-Tail Capture
- **Week 5:** Identify queries with high impressions but ranking on page 2 (positions 11–20) in GSC.
- **Week 6:** Enhance Worked Examples and FAQs on those underperforming tool pages with specific user-requested coordinate pairs and use cases.
- **Week 7:** Expand State Hub pages with additional county-level demographic and geographic centroid metrics.
- **Week 8:** Outreach to GIS educators, academic portals, and cartography resource hubs featuring the free blank SVG vector maps and methodology whitepapers.

### Days 61–90: Authority Building & Study Promotion
- **Week 9:** Release Part 2 of the Urban Detour Factor Study (focusing on European and Asian metropolitan grid networks).
- **Week 10:** Create interactive embeddable widgets for third-party blogs and research articles.
- **Week 11:** Launch community feature requests survey and update tool preset libraries.
- **Week 12:** Full quarterly review of search visibility, tool usage metrics, and organic domain rating.

---

## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Search Metrics:** Total organic impressions, organic clicks, average ranking position across priority keyword clusters.
- **Engagement Metrics:** Time on page, tool calculation executions per session, vector map downloads, CSV study downloads.
- **Quality & Reliability:** 99.99% uptime, <0.01% client-side error rate, 0 telemetry data leakage.
