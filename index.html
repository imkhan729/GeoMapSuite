Scientific Precision & Data Ethics

Why Professionals & Educators Choose GeoMap Suite

Engineered from the ground up for cartographic accuracy, user privacy, and cross-platform GIS interoperability.

True Geodesic Ellipsoidal Math

Flat Web Mercator maps distort distance and area by over 400% near the poles. GeoMap Suite implements Karney algorithms on the WGS84 ellipsoid for sub-millimeter geodesy.

Zero Tracking Client-Side Execution

Point-in-polygon checks, coordinate conversions, and buffer calculations run in your device’s memory. Your location, addresses, and files are never stored on tracking servers.

Official Public Data Foundations

Strictly built on verified public-domain cartography: US Census Bureau TIGER/Line, OpenStreetMap routable networks, Natural Earth, and Copernicus GLO-90 DEM elevation.

Seamless GIS & Vector Exports

Export calculations and boundaries directly to GeoJSON (RFC 7946), KML, CSV coordinates, scalable SVG outlines, and high-resolution PNGs for QGIS, ArcGIS, or graphic design.
